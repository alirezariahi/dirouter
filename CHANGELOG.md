## 1.1.0 (2026-11-25)

Sprint 4: everything v1.0.0 announced or showed in the interface but did not fully do is finished
or stated as it is, and the missing operator pages are added. Upgrading from 1.0.x:
[docs/upgrade-v1.1.md](docs/upgrade-v1.1.md); it lists the behaviour changes to review first.

- **Guardrails:** `secret` and `json_schema` policies are enforced (see Fixed below). New
  `moderation` policy type (an OpenAI-compatible moderation model or a Llama Guard chat model, with
  per-category thresholds, a timeout and fail-open or fail-closed) and `POST /v1/moderations`.
  Prompt-injection detection has English and Persian patterns, measured on a labelled set of 100
  samples, and an optional classifier model. The live test runs the gateway's own engine.
- **Translation:** structured output (`response_format`) on Anthropic and Bedrock, natively or
  through a forced tool. PDFs, other documents and audio input travel between OpenAI Chat,
  Responses, Anthropic, Gemini, Vertex and Bedrock instead of being dropped; a destination that
  cannot carry a part answers 400. `cache_control` breakpoints are kept on every block type and map
  to Bedrock `cachePoint` (verified against the mock provider's Bedrock route, not real Bedrock).
- **Providers and catalog:** Vertex AI service-account keys and Azure OpenAI Entra ID client
  credentials, with per-credential token caching and cooldown. The model catalog and prices sync
  from LiteLLM's public price list or an internal mirror in a worker job; price changes wait for
  approval by default. Pricing gains context-length tiers, a deprecation date and a batch discount
  (stored and shown only: there is no batch API, so the discount is not applied to any request).
- **MCP and A2A:** MCP resources, resource templates and prompts pass through the gateway; upstream
  servers protected by OAuth 2.1 work with client credentials or an authorization-code connection
  (API-only, no dashboard form yet). stdio servers stay outside the gateway behind the stdio bridge
  sidecar. A2A 1.0 streaming (`SendStreamingMessage`, `SubscribeToTask`) and signed push
  notifications; the streaming tests use a client written from the A2A specification, not the
  official A2A SDK.
- **Observability:** spans follow the OpenTelemetry GenAI semantic conventions v1.41.0, and metrics
  and logs can be exported over OTLP. Checked against the conventions' attribute names with a local
  collector, not with a real Langfuse or Phoenix backend. Request bodies can be captured per
  organization, project or key (off by default), masked, shown in the Log Explorer and replayed in
  the playground. The Live view streams over SSE with a polling fallback.
- **Dashboard and portal:** billing pages for wallets, payments, gateways, gift codes, pricing,
  plans, invoices and the profit report; an "MCP & agents" page; all seven routing strategies in
  the combo editor; team and project limits and budgets can be written and their usage read; the
  playground streams with tools, images and parameters. The portal verifies email addresses and has
  usage and invoice pages.
- **Billing:** invoices are Persian PDFs (right to left, Vazirmatn, Jalali dates, rials and tomans)
  with legal details and VAT on its own row (`tax.vatPercent`, default `0`). Postpaid sales are
  booked to the ledger when invoiced.
- **Identity and automation:** management API tokens and service accounts for CI and scripts;
  sign-in sessions can be listed and revoked, and revoked sessions stop their access tokens at once.
- **Alerts:** `breaker_open` and `queue_length` rules are evaluated; email channels send over SMTP.
- **Operations:** every image is built for linux/amd64 and linux/arm64 and published as one signed
  manifest list. js-tiktoken is kept in the control-api image.

**Fixed: guardrail types that v1.0.0 did not enforce.** In v1.0.0 a `secret` or `json_schema`
policy could be created and passed the live test in the dashboard, but the gateway skipped both
types without a word, so such policies never blocked, redacted or flagged traffic. 1.1.0 enforces
both on requests, answers and streams, validates `json_schema` schemas when they are saved, and
logs and counts (`dirouter_gateway_guardrail_unknown_total`) any policy of a type the gateway does
not know instead of ignoring it. Review existing `secret` and `json_schema` policies with
`action: block` before upgrading: they start blocking as soon as the new gateway runs.

**Security.** Fixes from the sprint 4 review:

- A blocking whole-answer guardrail (`json_schema`, `moderation`, an injection classifier) holds
  every stream event until the answer is complete, so text after a tool call or in a later block
  is judged too; an answer too long to judge is blocked.
- MCP connection tests, live MCP upstream calls and A2A proxying go through the SSRF guard and no
  longer follow redirects.
- MCP OAuth discovery uses protected resource metadata only when it names the server, follows a
  `resource_metadata` hint only on the server's origin, and keeps the discovered authorization
  server pinned.
- Four-eyes approval with service accounts: a change request made with a management token records
  the person behind the token, who cannot approve it, and neither can the creator of the requesting
  service account.
- Email-verification gates: creating service accounts needs a verified address, keys created with
  a management token check the token's creator, and users who accept an invitation that was not
  delivered by email start unverified when SMTP is configured.
- Alert channel tests are limited to one per channel per minute and ten per organization per hour,
  an email test needs a verified sender, and its subject is fixed.
- CSV exports prefix text cells that a spreadsheet would run as a formula.
- Also: `TRUST_PROXY` (default one hop) replaces trusting every proxy for the client address; open
  live streams re-check their session every 30 seconds and are capped at five per user;
  organization administrators no longer see a member's session addresses or devices; Vertex and
  Entra ID token exchanges run on their own timeout.

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-23)

The first stable release. Upgrading from 0.x: [docs/upgrade-v1.md](docs/upgrade-v1.md).

- **Gateway (sprints 1–2):** OpenAI, Anthropic, Responses and Gemini APIs on one endpoint; routing
  strategies, fallback, retries, hedging, circuit breakers shared across replicas, rate limits,
  budgets, exact and semantic caching, guardrails, distributed tracing, zero-downtime drains.
- **Agents and media (sprint 3):** MCP gateway and admin MCP server, A2A, auto routing, coding
  agent profiles, images, speech and transcription, provider plugins.
- **Billing (sprint 3):** prepaid wallets on a double-entry ledger, online top-ups, gift codes, sale
  prices and plans, invoices, a self-service portal.
- **Enterprise (sprints 2–3):** OIDC and SAML sign-in, SCIM provisioning, MFA and passkeys, custom
  roles, audit log, four-eyes approval for sensitive changes.
- **Operations (sprint 3):** signed images with SBOMs and CI vulnerability gates, threat model,
  SSRF protection for tenant URLs, HA compose, Helm chart, documentation site, v1 benchmark and
  ADR-0007.

**Corrections (added 2026-11-25).** A review after the release found that parts of the list above
were incomplete in 1.0.0. The real status in 1.0.0, each fixed in 1.1.0 unless noted:

- Guardrails: `secret` and `json_schema` policies were not enforced by the gateway; there was no
  moderation; prompt-injection detection was a few English regular expressions.
- Translation: `response_format` was dropped on the Anthropic and Bedrock paths; PDF, document and
  audio input was dropped; `cache_control` was kept only on text and never reached Bedrock.
- Routing and budgets: the gateway had all seven routing strategies, but the combo editor offered
  three; team and project limits and budgets were read by the gateway but nothing could write them.
- Distributed tracing used custom attribute names and exported traces only.
- MCP gateway: tools only; resources and prompts were empty and upstream OAuth was not supported.
  A2A had no streaming or push notifications.
- Billing was API-only: the admin dashboard had no billing pages. Invoice PDFs could not render
  Persian and had no tax field. The self-service portal had no email verification and no usage or
  invoice pages.
- SSRF protection did not cover MCP connection tests, live MCP upstream calls or A2A proxying.
- Alerts: `breaker_open` and `queue_length` rules were refused, and email went only through an HTTP
  relay.
- Signed images were published for linux/amd64 only.
- The model catalog was a fixed list in the release, with no sync from an external source.
- The gateway is plain Fastify, not NestJS as the technology table and ADR-0001 said (not a
  defect; ADR-0001 now carries an update).

Gateway with OpenAI- and Anthropic-compatible APIs, routing and fallback, virtual keys, the
